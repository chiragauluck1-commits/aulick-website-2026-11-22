Brand & Creative Studio

Make the value visible. Make the message memorable.

AULUCK’s Brand & Creative Studio brings brand identity, design and content production together. The work begins with understanding what the business needs to communicate, then selecting a visual and verbal direction that supports that message across digital and physical touchpoints.

Capabilities

  • Brand strategy, logo and visual identity systems.
  • Brand guidelines, creative direction and launch assets.
  • Social media designs, carousels, advertising creatives and presentations.
  • Brochures, catalogues, flyers, banners and print-ready artwork.
  • Video editing, promotional videos, motion graphics and AI-assisted creative production.

Standard and approach

Every deliverable should have a purpose, a defined audience, an agreed format and a clear review process. Visual impact matters, but it must support readability, brand consistency, accessibility and the action the audience is meant to take.

AI Studio

Where AI becomes production.

AULUCK’s AI Studio applies AI tools to creative production and workflow design. The focus is to use technology for a defined purpose: explore a visual direction, accelerate a production workflow, build an assistant for a real process or support a larger creative project. Human review remains essential for quality, context, accuracy and rights.

Capabilities

  • AI video creation and AI-assisted visual production.
  • AI short films and cinematic workflows.
  • AI full-movie production planning and production support.
  • AI music creation.
  • AI chatbot development and maintenance.
  • AI-assisted workflow automation.

Standard and approach

Before production, confirm the creative brief, rights to source assets, permissions for identifiable likenesses or voices, intended use, platform constraints and any disclosure requirements. Outputs require quality review before publication.
