Podcast production for clients

AULUCK may support organisations and creators that want to plan, produce and distribute a podcast or interview series. Scope can include format planning, episode structure, guest research and coordination, recording and production planning, audio and video editing, short-form clipping, podcast branding, YouTube channel management and content distribution.

Client production workflow

  1. Clarify the audience, purpose, format and publishing cadence.
  2. Plan episode themes, guest criteria, questions and production logistics.
  3. Record or receive source footage and audio through the agreed workflow.
  4. Edit the main episode and create approved short-form assets.
  5. Prepare metadata, thumbnails, captions and platform-specific versions.
  6. Publish, distribute and review performance against agreed measures.
