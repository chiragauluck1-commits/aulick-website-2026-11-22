        FAQ

        Frequently asked questions

        Is branding the same as marketing?

        No. Branding shapes identity, meaning, recognition and expectations. Marketing is the broader system of understanding demand, creating value, communicating an offer and reaching customers. Branding supports marketing, but the two are not interchangeable.

        Is digital marketing only social media?

        No. Digital marketing includes search, websites, analytics, email, paid media, social platforms, marketplaces, CRM and other digital touchpoints. Social media is one channel within a wider customer journey.

        What should a business do first: logo, website or marketing?

        Start with strategic clarity. Define the audience, problem, offer and positioning. Then build the identity and digital assets that communicate that strategy. The right order depends on the business and its current assets.

        How long does it take to build a brand?

        A visual identity can be produced within an agreed project schedule. Brand strength takes longer because memory, trust and reputation develop through repeated exposure and customer experience. Separate asset-production timelines from long-term market perception.

        Why can a business get reach but not sales?

        Reach is an attention measure. Sales also depend on relevance, offer quality, trust, price, timing, friction, lead quality and the sales process. Review the full journey rather than assuming more reach will solve the problem.

        Do I need paid ads if organic content is working?

        Not automatically. Organic and paid channels have different roles. The right mix depends on the objective, economics, audience behaviour and maturity of the offer. Advertising should be tested with a clear budget and reliable tracking.

        What is a marketing funnel?

        A funnel describes the journey from awareness and consideration to conversion and retention. It helps a business identify where suitable customers are getting stuck and what should be improved.

        Where does psychology fit into digital marketing?

        Psychology helps explain attention, perception, trust, comparison, decision friction and memory. It should be applied ethically to improve clarity and relevance, not to deceive or pressure customers.

        Will AI replace digital marketers?

        AI can accelerate parts of research, production, analysis and operations. It does not remove the need for market judgement, positioning, commercial thinking, verification, creativity and relationship management.

        Does AULUCK guarantee SEO rankings or sales?

        No. Search rankings, reach, leads and sales depend on multiple factors outside any one provider’s control. AULUCK should agree scope, measurement and reporting, but must not guarantee a fixed outcome.

        Can I request only one service?

        Yes. A project can be scoped around an individual service or a combination of capabilities where the requirements justify it.

        How is pricing decided?

        Starting prices are on the Pricing page. Services with a fixed starting price show it there, and services that need a scope are marked Get Quote. Prices are to be confirmed by AULUCK. Quotes depend on scope, deliverables, complexity, timeline and third-party costs.

        Are ad spend and third-party costs included?

        They are separate unless the written quotation specifically includes them. This may include media spend, influencer fees, printing, domain, hosting, software, APIs, subscriptions and other external costs.

        Are AULUCK courses currently open for enrolment?

        Course materials and course portfolio are reported prepared. Current enrolment status, launch dates, fees, course format and certificate policy must be confirmed on the relevant course page before a learner registers.
