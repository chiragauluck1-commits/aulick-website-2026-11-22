Pricing

Transparent scope. Clear deliverables. Written quotations.

Every project is quoted in writing after we understand the scope, deliverables and timeline.

Custom quotations

For services without a published starting price, AULUCK prepares a custom quotation after understanding the business, deliverables, complexity, timeline, dependencies and third-party costs. Share your brief to receive a scope that is specific to your requirements.

Request a Custom Quote

Costs that may be separate

Unless included in a written quotation, these are separate:

  • Advertising spend and influencer payouts.
  • Printing, media buying and outdoor site costs.
  • Domain registration, hosting, premium software, APIs and subscriptions.
  • Travel and other third-party costs.

What a quotation will state

  • Scope, deliverables and timeline.
  • Payment schedule and revision rounds.
  • Client responsibilities, ownership and handover terms.
  • Third-party charges and reporting cadence.
  • Acceptance criteria. A signed agreement or final written quotation governs the engagement.
